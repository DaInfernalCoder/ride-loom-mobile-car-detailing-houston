# Ride Loom design direction

## Subject, audience, and job

Mobile detailing for Houston drivers who want the cabin, wheels, glass, and paint treated as connected parts of one vehicle. The page should make that whole-vehicle view clear and lead to calling or booking once contact details are confirmed.

## Visual system

- Deep orbit — `#11202C`
- Midnight blue — `#203B52`
- Ceramic — `#E7ECEA`
- White light — `#FAFCFB`
- Orbital cyan — `#4CB7C5`
- Solar copper — `#D98B52`
- Display: Archivo Black
- Body: Manrope
- Utility: IBM Plex Mono

## Layout thesis

Reuse the editorial/service-process frame and convert the hero curve into a single orbital path connecting cabin, wheels, glass, and bodywork.

## Signature

One asymmetric orbital line passes through four service touchpoints; it encodes whole-vehicle attention rather than space decoration.

## Critique

The first concept used too many space motifs. The revision removes stars and gradients, keeping only one functional orbit on a ceramic-and-blue automotive surface.
